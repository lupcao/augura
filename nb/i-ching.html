    Kommer snart

    Slik fungerer det

    • Hver linje bruker metodens faktiske odds: tre mynter (2, 6, 6, 2 av 16) eller ryllikstilker (1, 5, 7, 3 av 16).
    • Rettferdig: hvert resultat kommer fra den sikre tilfeldighetsgeneratoren i nettleseren din (crypto.getRandomValues), uten skjevhet.
    • Privat: ingenting du skriver eller trekker, forlater denne siden. Ingen cookies, ingen sporing.

    Worked example

    The sample has one changing line. The first hexagram describes the situation, the changing line is the part to read closely, and the second hexagram shows how the situation could change. Read it as a prompt for reflection, not a forecast.

    Options

    Choose three coins or yarrow stalks as the method and type a question first if you like. The result names your hexagram, any changing lines and the hexagram they turn it into.

    Real uses

    Think through a decision, start a journal entry, learn the 64 hexagrams by casting a few, or add a quiet ritual to your study of the classic text.

    How the randomness works

    Six lines are cast one after another, each an independent draw from the secure generator mapped to the odds of the chosen method. Two of the four outcomes are changing lines; with yarrow stalks the changing yang line is three times as likely as the changing yin line, with coins they are equally likely.
