Short description

Flip a coin, spin the wheel, roll dice or draw a card from your widget, Control Center or Apple Watch. Every result random, nothing tracked, no ads, no account.

Long description

Augura is a calm, private toolbox for chance. Flip a coin, roll dice, spin a wheel, pick a number or a name, draw a tarot card, cast the I Ching or pull a rune. Every result is truly random, and nothing you draw ever leaves your iPhone.

DECIDE

  • Coin, yes or no, dice, numbers, letters, colors and dates
  • A list picker with a decision wheel: type your options and let Augura choose
  • Rock-paper-scissors, random playing cards and random names
  • Map Point: a random place on the map
  • Contact and Picture: a random person from your address book or a random photo from your library, picked on your device only

SEEK

  • Tarot with the full 78-card deck and reversals
  • I Ching with three coins or yarrow stalks, with the changing lines read for you
  • Runes, Ogham, Lenormand, geomancy, omikuji fortune slips, tea leaves, knucklebones and dominoes
  • Fal-e Hafez, Sortes Virgilianae, the Greek alphabet oracle, augury, Korean yut sticks, the language of flowers, New Year wax pouring, the paper fortune teller, the Old Turkic Irk Bitig, the Ling Qi Jing and the ancient Sortes Astrampsychi
  • A learning wiki for every tradition: where it comes from, how it is read, what each card, rune or hexagram means
  • Presented for reflection and entertainment, not as prediction

TOGETHER

  • Party games for one phone and a group: Fingers (pick a player), Impostor, Forehead, Hat game, Pass it, Letters & categories, World quiz, Chain, Scavenger hunt, Word grid
  • A scoreboard and turn timer for any game night

WORLD

  • A World Heritage site, where the sun is rising right now, tonight's night sky, an element of the day
  • Dinosaurs and fossils, a real earthquake from the last hours, a rock, gem or meteorite
  • An exoplanet, a place on the Moon or Mars, and the next asteroid flying past Earth

DISCOVER

  • A random song, book, podcast, artwork, space photo, poem, website or video
  • Real coins, historic card art and public-domain artwork, always with credit

ALWAYS AT HAND

  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, including a one-tap button widget
  • Controls for Control Center and the Lock Screen (iOS 18)
  • Apple Watch app and complications
  • Siri voice phrases for 10 modes; every mode in Shortcuts and, on iOS 18+, Spotlight
  • History and notes for every draw, saved lists, a daily draw reminder

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
  • Draws, history and settings stay on your device
  • Most of Augura works offline; about twenty modes (such as Song, Podcast, Film, Book, Space, Rocks and gems, Species, Earthquake, Wikipedia, YouTube and Fair draw) ask their outside service directly, with no Augura server and no account
  • Contact names and small photos used by widgets stay on your device and are deleted when you turn off access in Settings
