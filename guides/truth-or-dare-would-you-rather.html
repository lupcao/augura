Truth or dare and would-you-rather questions

Family-friendly truth or dare and would-you-rather questions to draw in your browser, plus charades, drawing words and story dice in the iPhone app. This page has a free browser tool at the top and a guide to the Party mode in the Augura iPhone app. The browser tool is simpler than the app mode: there is no web version of the full mode.

Draw a truth, a dare or a would-you-rather

Press Truth, Dare or Would you rather to draw one card. Everything here is meant for people in the same room.

What this tool does

  • The decks are the family-friendly starting decks of the app's Party mode: 150 truths, 150 dares and 300 would-you-rather questions. The optional packs, such as couples' questions, are not here.
  • Each press draws one card from the deck you chose. A card does not come back until every card of that deck has been used; then the deck is shuffled again, and its first card is never the one you just saw.
  • Anyone can pass or ask for another card. Choose what suits the people in the room.
  • Everything runs in this page on your device with the browser's secure random generator. No request is sent after the page loads, no account is needed and nothing is saved: closing or reloading the tab clears it.

These decks are on from the start. Optional packs, off until you switch them on in the gear sheet: Couples' questions, drawing prompts, writing prompts, photo prompts, conversation starters.

How to use it

Choose Would you rather, Truth or dare, Icebreakers, Charades, Drawing words, or Story dice. Each draw stays inside the chosen section and comes from the family-friendly pool that starts on.

Story dice

Story dice are three 3D dice with a small picture on every face. Augura selects the three pictures with the system secure random generator before the dice tumble, and with Reduce Motion they simply show the result. Make a story from the three ideas in any order; there is no right answer and no scoring.

Keeping it kind

Choose prompts that suit the people present. Anyone can pass, change a prompt, or stop playing. Augura does not show adult material through the starting examples or sharing defaults.

Packs and reset

The gear sheet lets you switch individual packs on or off, including couples’ questions and drawing, writing, photo, and conversation prompts. Reset party packs restores the supplied family-friendly starting packs. It never enables a future adult pack.

Where it comes from

The prompts are the decks supplied with the app, listed above; the browser tool above uses the truth, dare and would-you-rather decks of them. The story-dice pictures are picked with the system random generator before the dice tumble.

What it cannot do

  • It is not an adult or 18+ question generator. The starting decks are family-friendly, and the optional packs, such as couples' questions, are off until you switch them on.
  • The browser tool has the three decks only: no icebreakers, charades, drawing words or story dice, which are in the app.
  • Story dice have no right answer and no scoring: they are a starting point for a story.
  • It cannot know who is in the room. Choose prompts that suit the people present: anyone can pass, change a prompt or stop playing.
