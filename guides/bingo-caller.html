Bingo caller

Call every ball once from a 75- or 90-ball pool for a local bingo game. This page has a free browser tool at the top and a guide to the Bingo caller mode in the Augura iPhone app (coming soon to the App Store). The browser tool is simpler than the app mode: there is no web version of the full mode.

Call bingo numbers here

Choose 75 or 90 balls and press Call next ball. Each number is called once, and the board marks every call.

Press Call next ball

    What this tool does

    • With 75 balls each call also shows its letter: B 1 to 15, I 16 to 30, N 31 to 45, G 46 to 60, O 61 to 75. The pool is fixed from the first call until you start a new game.
    • After the last number the caller stops. New game asks you to press it twice, so a stray tap cannot clear the board.
    • It makes no cards, does not check for a winner and does not speak aloud; a screen reader announces each new call.
    • Everything runs in this page on your device with the browser's secure random generator. No request is sent after the page loads, no account is needed and nothing is saved: closing or reloading the tab clears it.

    What it is

    Choose 75 balls or 90 balls before the first call. The caller uses the usual numbered pools, but it does not make cards or check a winner.

    How to use it

    1. One ball at a time. Call next ball picks one unused number securely before the ball appears. The latest call is also spoken to VoiceOver.
    2. Finish or reset. Every ball is called once, then the caller stops. The game stays saved if you leave and return. Reset asks before it clears the board.

    How the calls work

    Call the next ball

    Tap Call next ball. Augura picks one remaining ball with the system secure random generator before the display animates it. The latest call is announced for VoiceOver, and the board marks every number already called.

    No repeats, even after a relaunch

    The chosen pool and its call order are saved on this device. A ball can appear only once; after all 75 or 90 numbers are called, the caller stops. Use Reset only when you have agreed to start a new game; it asks before clearing the board.

    Where it comes from

    Each ball is picked with the system random generator on your iPhone before the display shows it. The pool and the call order are stored on the device only.

    What it cannot do

    • It makes no bingo cards and does not check whether someone has won. Players need their own cards.
    • It does not speak the numbers aloud for everyone: only VoiceOver announces the latest call, for people who use it.
    • It has no themed, musical or picture bingo, and no tickets.

    Bingo caller is a simple local score and caller tool. It has no money, betting, entry fee, prize, or gambling mechanics.
