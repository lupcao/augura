Guides to what Augura can do

What each mode of the Augura iPhone app does, how to use it, where its content comes from and what it cannot do. These guides are in English for now.

  • Random Name Picker and Name GeneratorOne to ten random first, last or full names from seven countries, or made-up fantasy, sci-fi and place names.
  • Secret Santa GeneratorDraw gift partners with one-way exclusions, then pass the phone so each person sees only their own match.
  • Tournament Bracket GeneratorRandom seeding for 2 to 32 entrants, single or double elimination, and a saved bracket you advance by tapping winners.
  • Bingo CallerCall every ball once from a 75- or 90-ball pool, with a board that marks what has been called.
  • Truth or Dare and Would You Rather QuestionsFamily-friendly truth or dare and would you rather in your browser, plus icebreakers, charades, drawing words and story dice in the app.
  • Random Password, PIN and Passphrase GeneratorA passphrase, PIN, password or UUID from the secure random generator, made in your browser or in the app and never saved.
  • What to Eat? Random Food PickerOne random food from a list of 27, filtered by vegan, vegetarian, pescatarian or omnivore and by category.
  • Score KeeperTwo to eight players, +1, +5 and −1 buttons, Undo, a random first player and a turn timer.
  • Yut Sticks FortuneFour yut sticks thrown three times, one of 64 traditional phrases, read as an image to reflect on.
  • Ogham ReadingDraw one of the 20 ogham letters, or three, with its tree and a short reading.
  • Tea Leaf ReadingPour a virtual cup: three to five symbols land in the rim, side and bottom, to read as a gentle story.
