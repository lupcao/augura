Score keeper for game night

Keep score for any game with big +1, +5 and −1 buttons, Undo, a random first player and a turn timer that moves round the table. This page has a free browser tool at the top and a guide to the Scoreboard mode in the Augura iPhone app (coming soon to the App Store). The browser tool is simpler than the app mode: there is no web version of the full mode.

Keep score here

Choose 2 to 8 players, type their names and press +1, +5 or −1 next to a name.

Players4

    What this tool does

    • Undo takes back the last 50 changes. Random first player picks one of the players with equal chance. Reset scores sets everyone back to zero (Undo can bring the scores back).
    • There is no turn timer in the browser version (the app has one), and scores are not shared between devices.
    • Scores are for fun and are not about money, bets or prizes.
    • Everything runs in this page on your device with the browser's secure random generator. No request is sent after the page loads, no account is needed and nothing is saved: closing or reloading the tab clears it.

    What it is

    A scoreboard for board games, card games, quizzes and backyard games. It works for two to eight players, needs no account and keeps your scores on this phone, even after you close the app.

    How to use it

    1. Choose how many players there are and type their names. Without a name, a player is called Player 1, Player 2 and so on.
    2. Press +1, +5 or −1 next to a name to change that score. Undo takes back the last change, up to fifty times.
    3. Press Random first player to let Augura pick who starts.
    4. Press Start the timer to give the highlighted player a turn. Next player passes the turn on and starts the timer again. When time runs out the phone gives a soft buzz and shows that time is up.
    5. Press Save the scores to keep the current scores in History, or Share to send them to your friends.

    Options

    • Players: two to eight.
    • Turn length: 30, 60 or 120 seconds, in the mode's settings.

    Reset scores sets everyone back to zero.

    Fairness and privacy

    The first player is chosen with the device's secure random generator before it is shown. Names and scores stay on this phone and are never uploaded. Only a drawn first player or scores you choose to save go to your on-device History. Scores are just for fun and are never about money, bets or prizes.

    Where it comes from

    The scoreboard and the turn timer are Augura's own code. No third-party data is used.

    What it cannot do

    • It handles two to eight players. It is a score counter, not a sports scoreboard for a screen, a league table or a rules engine for tennis or volleyball.
    • It does not sync scores between phones: everything stays on this iPhone.
    • It does not know the rules of your game: you press +1, +5 or −1 yourself.
