Secret Santa generator

Make a local gift draw and reveal one private match at a time by passing the phone. This page has a free browser tool at the top and a guide to the Secret Santa mode in the Augura iPhone app (coming soon to the App Store). The browser tool is simpler than the app mode: there is no web version of the full mode.

Draw Secret Santa names here

Type everyone's name, one per line, add any rules and press Draw. Then pass this device round: each person taps their own name, sees only their own match and hides it again.

The draw is done. Pass this device round: each person taps only their own name.

    What this tool does

    • Everyone gives to exactly one person and receives from exactly one. Nobody draws themselves or a person you ruled out. If no draw can follow your rules, the tool says so instead of breaking one.
    • 2 to 50 different names (capital letters do not matter, and a name cannot contain > or =).
    • A person can tap their name again until you press Start over, so hand the device only to the person whose turn it is. Switching to another tab or app hides a shown match.
    • Matches are not emailed, texted or shared; there is no wish list.
    • Everything runs in this page on your device with the browser's secure random generator. No request is sent after the page loads, no account is needed and nothing is saved: closing or reloading the tab clears it.

    What it is

    Secret Santa mode draws one gift partner for each person in your group, with any exclusions you set.

    The app makes a derangement: nobody draws themself, every person gives to one person, and every person receives once. If the exclusions make that impossible, it explains that you need to remove an exclusion or add someone instead of quietly breaking a rule.

    How to use it

    1. Add the group. Enter every person taking part. Repeated names stay separate, so use a detail if two people have the same name.
    2. Respect exclusions. Add one-way exclusions for partners or last year’s pairs. Add both directions when neither person should draw the other.
    3. Pass the phone. After the draw, each person taps only their own name and sees one match. Tap Hide and pass phone before the next person. Matches are not shared or saved in history.

    Every person gives once and receives once, nobody draws themselves, and nobody draws the partner they are excluded from. Each of the four then opens only their own line, on the phone you pass round.

    Who sees what

    Pass the phone privately

    After the draw, pass the phone to one person at a time. They tap only their own name, see only their match, then tap Hide and pass phone before the next turn. There is no share card, history entry, widget, or public link for this mode.

    Keep it on this device

    The setup and assignment stay on this device. When the app leaves the foreground, any shown match is hidden and the app-switcher snapshot is covered. Start over to change the names or exclusions and make a new draw.

    Where it comes from

    The draw runs on your iPhone with the system random generator. The group, the exclusions and the result are not sent to any server, and no account is needed.

    What it cannot do

    • It does not send matches by email, text message or WhatsApp. The reveal happens on one phone, in person: that is why no emails are needed.
    • It has no wish lists and no shared group across several devices.
    • Once the phone has been passed on, a match cannot be looked up again: nothing is saved to history or shared.
    • It will not break a rule to finish: if the exclusions leave someone with no possible match, it tells you to remove an exclusion or add a person.
