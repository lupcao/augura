Tournament bracket generator

Randomly seed a small single- or double-elimination tournament and tap each winner as you play. This page has a free browser tool at the top and a guide to the Tournament mode in the Augura iPhone app (coming soon to the App Store). The browser tool is simpler than the app mode: there is no web version of the full mode.

Make a bracket here

Type 2 to 32 names, one per line, and press Make bracket. The names are shuffled once into seeds. Then tap the winner of each match.

What this tool does

  • This browser version is single elimination only (the app also has double elimination). When the number of names is not a power of two, some names get a bye and go straight to the next round; the shuffle decides who.
  • Tap a winner again to undo. Changing a winner clears the later matches that depended on it.
  • 2 to 32 different names. There is no printable sheet, export or round-robin.
  • Everything runs in this page on your device with the browser's secure random generator. No request is sent after the page loads, no account is needed and nothing is saved: closing or reloading the tab clears it.

What it is

Enter 2 to 32 names. Augura uses the system secure random generator to set the seeds once, then keeps that draw when you leave and return. Empty places in a non-power-of-two field are explicit byes.

How to use it

  1. Add the entrants. Enter 2 to 32 names, one per line. Augura securely randomizes their seeds once and saves the bracket.
  2. Choose the route. Choose single elimination for one-loss-out play, or double elimination. In double elimination, one loss moves a player from the upper route to the lower route; a second loss eliminates them.
  3. Settle the final. The undefeated finalist must lose twice. If the lower-route finalist wins the grand final, Augura adds a reset final. Correcting an earlier result clears later choices while the original seeds stay put.

Tap a player to advance them from a match. If you correct an earlier winner, later matches that depend on it are cleared so you can choose them again.

Where it comes from

The seeding uses the system random generator on your iPhone, once, when you create the bracket. The bracket is saved on the iPhone and kept when you leave and come back.

What it cannot do

  • It holds 2 to 32 entrants, and it organizes a bracket only. It has no round-robin, group stage or league table.
  • It has no pictures or logos for entrants, no printable sheet and no spreadsheet export.
  • It does not play the games for you: you tap the winner of each match.

Tournament is a simple local organizer for games and activities. It does not run contests, handle entry fees, or award prizes.
