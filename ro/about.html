About Augura

O trusă calmă și privată pentru tot ce lăsați la voia întâmplării. Creat de Luca.

Augura at a glance

What it isA calm, private iPhone app for chance: a coin, dice, a decision wheel, random numbers and names, party games, and traditional oracles such as tarot, the I Ching and runes.
PriceFree today. An optional one-time Plus is planned, and the core tools are meant to stay free.
PlatformeiPhone (iOS 17 or later), with an Apple Watch app, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and Control Center controls on iOS 18 or later. There is no Android version. Many tools also run free in your browser on this website.

Ads and privacyNo ads, no account, no analytics and no tracking.
LimbiThe app is in 27 languages. This website is in 27 languages.
DezvoltatorCreat de Luca.
Built with AILuca directs the project and decides what is released. AI coding assistants (Claude from Anthropic and, earlier, Codex from OpenAI) write the code. Most translations were made with AI too, and not every language has been checked by a fluent reader yet.

What Augura is for

Chance is an old and honest way to settle a small question, share out a turn or start a conversation. Augura aims to make that quick, fair and quiet: every result is chosen by the device's own random number generator before any animation plays, nothing needs an account, and nothing you draw is sent to the developer.

The tradition behind the oracles

Many draws in Augura come from old practices of asking a question and letting chance answer. Tarot began as a card game in 15th-century Italy and only later became a tool for reflection. The I Ching is an ancient Chinese book, read with three coins or yarrow stalks. The Elder Futhark is the oldest runic alphabet. Omikuji are paper fortune slips drawn at Japanese shrines and temples. Yut sticks are a Korean New Year custom, ogham is the early Irish tree alphabet, and reading tea leaves is a European custom. The app's wiki explains where each one comes from and how it is read.

Toate sunt prezentate pentru reflecție și distracție, nu ca predicții
