Six of Cups Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Cups

nostalgia, kindness, innocence

The card

In an old courtyard, a child hands a smaller child a cup filled with white flowers, and more flowering cups stand nearby. The scene feels like a warm memory. The card speaks of simple generosity and the comfort of the past, and invites you to bring that gentleness into the present.

Upright meaning

Sweet memories and simple kindness; the past or an old friend brings comfort.

Reversed meaning

Living in the past, idealizing what was, or struggling to grow beyond childhood patterns.
