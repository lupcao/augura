Snake Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 7 of 36 · playing-card insert Q♣

complication, detour, desire

Meaning

The Snake is the winding path: complications, detours and the need for cleverness. Traditionally it can also stand for a rival or someone with hidden motives, or for desire and temptation. It asks you to look carefully at what is not as simple as it seems.

In combination

It complicates its neighbor, adding a twist, a detour or a mixed motive.
