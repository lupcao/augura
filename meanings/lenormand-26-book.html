Book Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 26 of 36 · playing-card insert 10♦

secrets, knowledge, the unknown

Meaning

The Book holds what is not yet known: secrets, study, knowledge and things kept closed. Traditionally it is read as information still hidden, either from you or by you. It asks what you need to learn, or what is better left unopened for now.

In combination

It hides its neighbor or makes it a matter of study, something unknown, private or still to be learned.
