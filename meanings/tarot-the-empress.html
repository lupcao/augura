The Empress Tarot Card Meaning

Major Arcana, number III

abundance, nurture, creativity, nature

The card

A woman rests on soft cushions in a field of ripening wheat, with a stream and a forest behind her and a heart-shaped shield bearing the sign of Venus at her side. She wears a crown of stars and a gown patterned with pomegranates. The card invites you to tend what is growing in your life, to enjoy your senses and to let things ripen in their own time.

Upright meaning

Abundance, nurture, creativity.

Reversed meaning

Smothering, creative block.
