Tower Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 19 of 36 · playing-card insert 6♠

institution, authority, solitude

Meaning

The Tower stands for large institutions: offices, organizations, authority and official structures. Traditionally it also speaks of standing apart, of solitude, boundaries and a long view from high up. It asks where you need structure and where you have walled yourself in.

In combination

It makes its neighbor official or formal, or sets it apart behind walls.
