Sun Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 31 of 36 · playing-card insert A♦

success, energy, warmth

Meaning

The Sun is success, energy and warmth. Traditionally it is one of the most positive cards: confidence, vitality and things going well in full daylight. It asks you to step into the light and let yourself shine.

In combination

It brightens its neighbor, adding success, clarity and a positive turn.
