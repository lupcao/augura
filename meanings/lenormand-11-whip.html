Whip Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 11 of 36 · playing-card insert J♣

conflict, repetition, intensity

Meaning

The Whip is energy in motion: arguments, debate, repetition and physical effort. Traditionally it can mean tension that keeps coming back, but also exercise, discipline and passion. It asks where a pattern is repeating and how you might break it or put it to good use.

In combination

It adds friction or repetition to its neighbor, something argued over, done again or pursued with intensity.
