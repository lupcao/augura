The Moon Tarot Card Meaning

Major Arcana, number XVIII

illusion, dreams, the unconscious, uncertainty

The card

A full moon with a calm face in profile hangs between two towers, shedding drops of light over a winding path. A dog and a wolf howl up at it, and a crayfish crawls out of a pool in the foreground. The card speaks of uncertainty, dreams and half-seen fears, and asks you to move slowly and feel your way until the picture becomes clear.

Upright meaning

Illusion, intuition, the unconscious.

Reversed meaning

Confusion lifting, clarity emerging.
