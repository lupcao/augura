Heart Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 24 of 36 · playing-card insert J♥

love, affection, warmth

Meaning

The Heart is love and affection in every form: romance, kindness and what you care about. Traditionally it is read as warm feelings and emotional generosity. It asks you to follow what you truly love.

In combination

It warms its neighbor, showing it is loved, cared about or approached with feeling.
