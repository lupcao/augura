King of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Pentacles

abundance, security, discipline, generosity

The card

A king sits on a throne decorated with bulls' heads, his robe covered in grapevines, a coin resting in one hand and a scepter in the other, with a castle rising behind him. He has built lasting security through patience and good judgment. The card asks you to lead with steadiness and to share generously what you have built.

Upright meaning

Abundance through discipline; a secure, generous provider who has built something lasting.

Reversed meaning

Materialism, stubbornness or measuring worth only by money.
