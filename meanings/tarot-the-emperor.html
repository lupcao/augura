The Emperor Tarot Card Meaning

Major Arcana, number IV

structure, authority, stability, responsibility

The card

A bearded ruler sits on a stone throne carved with rams' heads, holding a scepter and an orb, with bare red mountains behind him and armor showing beneath his robe. He stands for order, protection and the rules that keep things running. The card asks where a clear structure or a firm boundary would help you, and where control has hardened into stiffness.

Upright meaning

Structure, authority, stability.

Reversed meaning

Rigidity, control issues.
