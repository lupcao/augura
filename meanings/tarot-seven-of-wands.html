Seven of Wands Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Wands

standing firm, perseverance, conviction

The card

A man on higher ground holds his wand across his body against six wands rising toward him from below. His mismatched footwear suggests he was caught by surprise, yet he keeps his footing. The card asks you to weigh what is truly worth defending and then hold your position with patience.

Upright meaning

You hold the high ground against pressure; stand by what you believe and keep defending it.

Reversed meaning

Feeling overwhelmed or worn down, you are tempted to give up ground you should keep.
