Hexagram 3: Difficulty at the Beginning

䷂ 屯 (Zhūn) · Chaotic beginnings. Persevere and accept help.

new beginnings, growing pains, perseverance, asking for help

Lower trigram: Thunder ☳ · Upper trigram: Water ☵

The judgment

Something new is pressing upward through confusion, like a sprout pushing through hard ground. Things feel tangled, but this is the mess of growth, not failure. Don't rush toward distant goals; keep going steadily, and find helpers who can bring order to the chaos.

The image

Thunder rumbles beneath heavy rain clouds: a storm is gathering before the rain breaks. In times like this, sort out the tangle thread by thread and put things in order.

The six lines

Read only the lines that changed in your casting, counting from the bottom.

  • Line 1 (bottom): An obstacle at the very start makes you hesitate. Don't push through blindly; hold to your aim and gather people who can help.
  • Line 2: Difficulties pile up, and an offer arrives that isn't quite right. Wait patiently for what truly fits, even if it takes a long time.
  • Line 3: Chasing the deer into the forest without a guide only gets you lost. Recognize when to stop rather than pressing on alone.
  • Line 4: You can't manage this alone, and help is offered. Accept the connection and move forward together; it goes well.
  • Line 5: You have good things to give, but they are hard to share right now. Small, careful steps work; a grand push would backfire.
  • Line 6 (top): Stuck and in tears, with the horses pulling apart. Don't stay in this despair; the sorrow eases once you look for another way.
