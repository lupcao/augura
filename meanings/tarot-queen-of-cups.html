Queen of Cups Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Cups

compassion, intuition, emotional care

The card

A queen sits on a carved throne at the very edge of the sea, gazing into an ornate, closed cup. She seems to listen inwardly to what the cup holds. The card invites you to care deeply for yourself and others, trusting your intuition while keeping healthy boundaries.

Upright meaning

Deep compassion and intuition; she listens closely and holds space for others' feelings.

Reversed meaning

Giving so much that you lose yourself, or emotions that overflow into dependence.
