Four of Wands Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Wands

celebration, home, stability

The card

Four wands stand in the foreground with a garland of flowers and fruit hung across them, like a gate or a canopy. Behind it, two figures lift bouquets in welcome, and a castle rises in the distance. The card marks a firm foundation worth celebrating, a moment to rest and enjoy what has been built before moving on.

Upright meaning

A joyful milestone: celebration, a welcoming home and the stability to enjoy what you have built.

Reversed meaning

The celebration feels strained or postponed, or home life lacks its usual harmony.
