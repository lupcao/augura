Tree Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 5 of 36 · playing-card insert 7♥

growth, roots, wellbeing

Meaning

The Tree is slow, steady growth with deep roots. Traditionally it speaks of wellbeing, family lines and the things that take years to mature, and it is read as patience rather than quick results. It asks what in your life needs time and care to grow strong.

In combination

It makes its neighbor slow-growing and long-lasting, something rooted that develops over time.
