Hexagram 54: The Marrying Maiden

䷵ 歸妹 (Guī Mèi) · Impulsive union. Mind how you enter commitments.

commitment, position, tact, patience

Lower trigram: Lake ☱ · Upper trigram: Thunder ☳

The judgment

You are entering an arrangement where you do not set the terms, as a young bride once entered a household not her own. Charging ahead or demanding more than your place allows will backfire. Act with tact, keep your expectations honest, and remember that every bond only lasts if it is built on more than impulse.

The image

Lake below and Thunder above: thunder stirs the lake, and the water moves where the excitement leads. Keep the long run in mind from the very first step, and you will recognize a passing stir for what it is.

The six lines

Read only the lines that changed in your casting, counting from the bottom.

  • Line 1 (bottom): You come in at a lower rank than you hoped. Like someone limping who still walks, you can make real progress through quiet, loyal effort.
  • Line 2: Like someone with one eye who still sees, you are let down by someone you counted on. Stay true to your own inner vision.
  • Line 3: Eager for a place, you are tempted to accept a lesser one that doesn't fit you. Better to wait than to lower yourself.
  • Line 4: You hold out past the expected time. That is fine: a bond worth having is often worth waiting for.
  • Line 5: Your quiet dignity matters more than outward display, like a bride dressed more plainly than her attendants. Like the moon just short of full, this modesty brings good fortune.
  • Line 6 (top): The basket is empty and the ritual is hollow, all form and no substance. Going through the motions brings nothing, so look for what is real.
