The Star Tarot Card Meaning

Major Arcana, number XVII

hope, renewal, serenity, inspiration

The card

Beneath one large eight-pointed star and seven smaller ones, a naked woman kneels by a pool, pouring water from one jug into the pool and from the other onto the land. A bird rests in a tree behind her. After a hard season, the card invites quiet hope and asks you to trust that you can heal and begin again.

Upright meaning

Hope, renewal, serenity.

Reversed meaning

Discouragement, lost faith.
