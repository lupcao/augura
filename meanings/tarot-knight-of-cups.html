Knight of Cups Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Cups

romance, invitation, following the heart

The card

A knight on a white horse rides calmly forward, holding a cup out in front of him like an offering. Small wings rise from his helmet and heels, and a stream runs through the land ahead. The card speaks of offers made from the heart, and asks you to follow your ideals while keeping your feet on the ground.

Upright meaning

A romantic invitation; follow your heart and offer what you feel.

Reversed meaning

Moodiness, unrealistic romance or promises the heart cannot keep.
