Raidho Rune Meaning

ᚱ · sound "r" · Freyr's ætt · name means riding, a journey

journey, rhythm, direction, timing

Upright meaning

Raidho is the road and the rhythm of traveling it: a trip, a move, or simply finding the right pace for a task. It asks where you are actually heading, and whether the way you are going fits the place you want to reach.

Reversed (merkstave)

Reversed, it can mirror a journey that feels stalled, plans that keep shifting, or a pace that is out of step with your life. Check the route before pushing harder.

Lore

The name means riding or a journey. All three rune poems cover it; the Anglo-Saxon one notes with some humor that riding seems easy to anyone sitting indoors and hard for the one covering the long miles, and both Norse poems spare a thought for the horse that does the work.
