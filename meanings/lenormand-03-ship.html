Ship Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 3 of 36 · playing-card insert 10♠

travel, distance, longing

Meaning

The Ship stands for travel, distance and the pull of somewhere else. It can be a journey, a connection with faraway places, or a longing for change that has not set sail yet. It asks where you would go if you let yourself leave the harbor.

In combination

It adds distance or movement to its neighbor, pointing to something far away, foreign or on its way somewhere.
