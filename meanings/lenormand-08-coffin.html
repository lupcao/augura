Coffin Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 8 of 36 · playing-card insert 9♦

ending, closure, rest

Meaning

The Coffin marks an ending: something closing, being put away or laid to rest. Traditionally it is one of the heaviest cards, and many readers today take it as the end of a phase rather than anything literal; endings clear the ground for what follows. It asks what you are ready to let go of.

In combination

It brings its neighbor to a close, showing something that is ending, stopping or being put away.
