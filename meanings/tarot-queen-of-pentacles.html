Queen of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Pentacles

nurture, practicality, abundance, warmth

The card

A queen sits in a flowering garden under an arch of roses, a coin cradled in her lap, her throne carved with fruit and goats' heads, while a rabbit sits in the grass in the corner. She is warm, sensible and at home in the physical world. The card speaks of caring for people and things in practical ways, and asks you to look after your own comfort as well as everyone else's.

Upright meaning

Practical, nurturing and generous; she creates comfort and a warm home for others.

Reversed meaning

Neglecting yourself while caring for everyone else, or smothering with care.
