Scythe Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 10 of 36 · playing-card insert J♦

decision, cutting, suddenness

Meaning

The Scythe cuts quickly and cleanly. Traditionally it is read as a sudden turn, a decisive cut or a harvest that has come due, and it carries a note of caution, like a blade you handle with care. It asks what needs a clear decision now.

In combination

It makes its neighbor sudden or sharp, a quick change or a decision that cuts something away.
