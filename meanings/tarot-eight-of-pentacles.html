Eight of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Pentacles

diligence, practice, mastery, careful work

The card

A craftsman sits at his bench, carefully hammering a design into a coin, while finished pieces hang on a post beside him and a town waits in the distance. He is absorbed, patient and getting better with every stroke. The card honors steady practice and asks you to give your full attention to the work in front of you.

Upright meaning

Steady practice and careful work; mastery is built one detail at a time.

Reversed meaning

Perfectionism, boredom with repetition or cutting corners on the work.
