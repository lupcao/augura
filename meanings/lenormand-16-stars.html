Stars Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 16 of 36 · playing-card insert 6♥

hope, guidance, clarity

Meaning

The Stars bring clarity, hope and a sense of direction after a dark stretch. Traditionally they stand for wishes, inspiration and things going as they should, and also for a great number of something. They ask you to keep your eye on what guides you.

In combination

It clarifies and multiplies its neighbor, adding hope, success or a sense of being on course.
