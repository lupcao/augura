Knight of Wands Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Wands

action, adventure, boldness

The card

A knight on a rearing horse rides through the desert, the plume on his helmet flickering like a flame and his tunic marked with salamanders. He holds his wand high and seems eager to be anywhere but standing still. The card celebrates daring and momentum, and asks you to act with energy while keeping enough care not to burn out.

Upright meaning

Bold, fast action and a hunger for adventure; you charge ahead with passion.

Reversed meaning

Impulsiveness, impatience or a project abandoned halfway through.
