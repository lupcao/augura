Moon Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 32 of 36 · playing-card insert 8♥

recognition, feelings, creativity

Meaning

The Moon stands for recognition, reputation and the emotional life. Traditionally it is read as honor and acclaim, and also as dreams, intuition and creative work. It asks how you want to be seen and what your feelings are telling you.

In combination

It adds feeling or recognition to its neighbor, something emotional, creative or noticed by others.
