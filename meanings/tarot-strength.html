Strength Tarot Card Meaning

Major Arcana, number VIII

courage, patience, compassion, gentleness

The card

A woman in white calmly holds the jaws of a lion, an infinity sign above her head and a chain of flowers linking her to the animal. There is no struggle here, only steady and gentle strength. The card asks you to meet what is wild in yourself or in others with patience rather than force.

Upright meaning

Courage, patience, compassion.

Reversed meaning

Self-doubt, raw emotion.
