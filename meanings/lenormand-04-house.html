House Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 4 of 36 · playing-card insert K♥

home, family, security

Meaning

The House is home: the place you live, the people under your roof and the feeling of being safe. Traditionally it also points to what is private and stable, the foundation the rest of life stands on. It asks you to look at what makes you feel at home and how well it is tended.

In combination

It brings the neighboring card indoors, into the home, the family or your private life.
