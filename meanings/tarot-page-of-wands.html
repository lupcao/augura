Page of Wands Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Wands

curiosity, enthusiasm, news

The card

A young figure in a tunic patterned with salamanders stands in a bare desert, gazing up at the tall wand in their hands. Distant pyramids sit on the horizon. The card brings a beginner's excitement, a fresh message or idea, and invites you to explore it without needing to be an expert yet.

Upright meaning

An eager messenger of new ideas; curiosity and excitement point toward an adventure.

Reversed meaning

Enthusiasm without follow-through, restless ideas, or news that disappoints.
