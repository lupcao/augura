Man Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 28 of 36 · playing-card insert A♥

a man, the reader, a partner

Meaning

The Man is one of the two person cards. Traditionally it represents the reader if he is a man, or an important man in the reader's life such as a partner, and many readers today simply choose the person card they identify with. The cards around it describe his situation and what is on his mind.

In combination

Any card beside it describes him or what he is facing, and traditionally the cards he faces show where his attention is going.
