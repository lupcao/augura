Anchor Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 35 of 36 · playing-card insert 9♠

stability, endurance, safe harbor

Meaning

The Anchor is stability: holding firm, staying put and reaching a safe harbor. Traditionally it is read as lasting security, perseverance and goals reached, and sometimes as being stuck. It asks what keeps you steady, and whether it is holding you or holding you back.

In combination

It makes its neighbor stable and lasting, something settled that stays put.
