Temperance Tarot Card Meaning

Major Arcana, number XIV

balance, moderation, patience, blending

The card

A winged angel stands with one foot on land and one in a pool, pouring water back and forth between two cups. A path leads from the water toward a crown of light on distant mountains, and irises bloom close by. The card invites you to find the middle way, combining different parts of your life with patience until they work together.

Upright meaning

Balance, moderation, patience.

Reversed meaning

Excess, imbalance.
