Two of Swords Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Swords

stalemate, a hard choice, avoidance, truce

The card

A blindfolded woman sits by the shore with two heavy swords crossed over her heart, a crescent moon above and rocks breaking the water behind her. She holds perfectly still, keeping both options at arm's length. The card asks what you are choosing not to look at, and whether this truce with yourself is a rest or a way of putting off a decision.

Upright meaning

A stalemate: you avoid a difficult decision by keeping your eyes covered.

Reversed meaning

The truce breaks and the decision can no longer wait, or information overload leaves you stuck.
