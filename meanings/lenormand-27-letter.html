Letter Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 27 of 36 · playing-card insert 7♠

message, document, writing

Meaning

The Letter is a written message: a note, an email, a document or anything put on paper. Traditionally it is communication that leaves a record, often more formal than words spoken aloud. It asks what needs to be written down or said clearly.

In combination

It puts its neighbor into writing, a message, a document or a piece of news on paper.
