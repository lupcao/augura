Wheel of Fortune Tarot Card Meaning

Major Arcana, number X

cycles, change, luck, turning point

The card

A great wheel marked with letters and symbols turns in the sky, with a sphinx seated on top, a snake sliding down one side and a jackal-headed figure rising on the other. In the four corners, winged creatures read from open books among the clouds. The card reminds you that life moves in cycles and asks how you can meet this turn with open eyes.

Upright meaning

Cycles, luck, a turning point.

Reversed meaning

Bad timing, resisting change.
