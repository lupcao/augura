Mice Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 23 of 36 · playing-card insert 7♣

loss, worry, wearing down

Meaning

The Mice nibble away: small losses, stress and things slowly wearing down. Traditionally they warn of something going missing or being eroded little by little. They ask what is quietly draining your time, energy or peace of mind.

In combination

It diminishes its neighbor, showing something shrinking, worn down or worried over.
