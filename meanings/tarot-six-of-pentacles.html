Six of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Pentacles

generosity, giving and receiving, fairness, sharing

The card

A well-dressed merchant holds a pair of scales in one hand and drops coins into the open palm of a kneeling person, while a second person waits beside them. Six coins hang in the air around the scene. The card looks at giving and receiving, and asks whether the exchanges in your life feel balanced, and on which side of the scales you stand today.

Upright meaning

Generosity flows; giving and receiving are in fair balance.

Reversed meaning

Strings attached to gifts, debt or a one-sided exchange of help.
