Bouquet Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 9 of 36 · playing-card insert Q♠

gift, joy, appreciation

Meaning

The Bouquet is a gift, a compliment or a pleasant surprise. Traditionally it stands for happiness, beauty, invitations and the warmth of being appreciated. It asks you to accept good things gracefully and to offer them to others.

In combination

It makes its neighbor pleasant and welcome, often something offered as a gift or an invitation.
