The Sun Tarot Card Meaning

Major Arcana, number XIX

joy, success, vitality, warmth

The card

A bright sun with a face shines over a garden wall lined with sunflowers. Below it, a naked child rides a white horse with arms open wide, carrying a red banner. The card celebrates warmth, clarity and simple happiness, and asks you to let yourself enjoy what is going well.

Upright meaning

Joy, success, vitality.

Reversed meaning

Temporary gloom, dimmed optimism.
