Woman Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 29 of 36 · playing-card insert A♠

a woman, the reader, a partner

Meaning

The Woman is one of the two person cards. Traditionally it represents the reader if she is a woman, or an important woman in the reader's life such as a partner, and many readers today simply choose the person card they identify with. The cards around it describe her situation and what is on her mind.

In combination

Any card beside it describes her or what she is facing, and traditionally the cards she faces show where her attention is going.
