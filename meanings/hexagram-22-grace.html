Hexagram 22: Grace

䷕ 賁 (Bì) · Grace. Form and beauty matter, but substance matters more.

beauty, form, simplicity, style

Lower trigram: Fire ☲ · Upper trigram: Mountain ☶

The judgment

Grace and beauty help small matters go smoothly. Style, manners and good presentation are real gifts, but they adorn what is already there and cannot replace it. Enjoy the beauty, and don't make big decisions on looks alone.

The image

A fire burns at the foot of the mountain, lighting its slopes beautifully but only a little way up. Use that light to bring order to everyday things, but don't settle serious disputes by appearances.

The six lines

Read only the lines that changed in your casting, counting from the bottom.

  • Line 1 (bottom): You make your feet graceful by leaving the comfortable carriage and walking. Choose the honest, simpler path over borrowed ease.
  • Line 2: Grace like a beard on a chin: it follows the face it grows on. Attend to what lies underneath before what decorates it.
  • Line 3: Everything feels graceful and glowing, almost too easy. Enjoy it, but stay steady and don't let comfort dull you.
  • Line 4: You wonder whether to choose polish or plain simplicity. Someone sincere arrives quickly, as if on a white horse, not as a threat but as a friend; choose what is genuine.
  • Line 5: You seek out a quiet, simple place and the people who matter, though your gift seems small. It may feel awkward, but sincerity outweighs show, and it ends well.
  • Line 6 (top): Plain, unadorned grace. When form and substance finally agree, nothing more is needed; no blame.
