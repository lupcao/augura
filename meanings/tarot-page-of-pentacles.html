Page of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Pentacles

study, a new venture, diligence, practical curiosity

The card

A young person stands in a green spring field, holding a single coin up in both hands and studying it closely, with freshly plowed earth and trees behind. They are earnest, careful and ready to learn. The card points to the start of a practical project or course of study, and to the value of small, steady first steps.

Upright meaning

A studious beginner ready to learn and to lay the groundwork for a real goal.

Reversed meaning

Procrastination, lack of focus or a plan that never leaves the notebook.
