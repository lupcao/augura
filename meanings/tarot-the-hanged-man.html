The Hanged Man Tarot Card Meaning

Major Arcana, number XII

surrender, pause, new perspective, letting go

The card

A man hangs upside down by one foot from a T-shaped cross of living wood, his other leg bent behind the first to form a figure four and his hands tucked behind his back. His face is calm and a halo glows around his head. The card asks whether a pause, or looking at things from a completely different angle, might show you what pushing ahead cannot.

Upright meaning

Surrender, a new perspective, a pause.

Reversed meaning

Stalling, needless sacrifice.
