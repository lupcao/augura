Death Tarot Card Meaning

Major Arcana, number XIII

endings, transformation, release, renewal

The card

A skeleton in black armor rides a white horse and carries a black banner marked with a white rose, while a king lies fallen and a child and a bishop turn to face him. On the horizon the sun shines between two towers. Rather than a literal death, the card speaks of a chapter closing so that something new can begin, and asks what you are ready to let go of.

Upright meaning

Endings, transformation, release.

Reversed meaning

Resisting change, stagnation.
