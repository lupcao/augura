Six of Swords Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Swords

transition, moving on, passage, calmer waters

The card

A ferryman poles a small boat across the water, carrying a hunched, cloaked figure and a child, with six swords standing upright in the bow. The water is choppy on one side and smooth on the other, and a quieter shore lies ahead. The card speaks of leaving a hard place behind, slowly and with help, carrying only what you need.

Upright meaning

Leaving troubled water for calmer shores; a necessary transition toward something better.

Reversed meaning

Resisting a needed change or carrying old baggage into the new place.
