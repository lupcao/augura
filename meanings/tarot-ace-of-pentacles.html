Ace of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Pentacles

opportunity, prosperity, a seed, groundedness

The card

A hand reaches out of a cloud offering a single golden coin above a flowering garden, where a path leads through an arch of hedges toward distant mountains. It is the seed of something real and tangible, a resource or a chance to build. The card invites you to notice the practical opening in front of you and to plant it with care.

Upright meaning

A tangible new opportunity: a seed of prosperity, a job or an investment to plant now.

Reversed meaning

A missed or delayed opportunity, or poor planning around money and resources.
