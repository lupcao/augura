Two of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Pentacles

balance, juggling, adaptability, priorities

The card

A young man in a tall hat dances as he juggles two coins inside a looping ribbon shaped like a figure eight, while ships rise and fall on big waves behind him. He keeps everything moving with good humor. The card speaks of balancing several demands at once and asks which of them truly need your hands right now.

Upright meaning

Juggling demands with grace; stay flexible and keep your priorities in balance.

Reversed meaning

Overcommitted and dropping balls; it is time to simplify and choose.
