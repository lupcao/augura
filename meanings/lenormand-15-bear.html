Bear Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 15 of 36 · playing-card insert 10♣

strength, protection, resources

Meaning

The Bear is strength and power: a strong, protective figure such as a boss or a mother, and the resources someone holds. Traditionally it is also read around appetite and reserves, what is kept and guarded. It asks how you use your own strength and whose protection you rely on.

In combination

It gives its neighbor weight and power, something strong, large or under someone's protection.
