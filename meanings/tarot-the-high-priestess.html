The High Priestess Tarot Card Meaning

Major Arcana, number II

intuition, mystery, stillness, inner knowing

The card

A woman sits calmly between a dark pillar and a light one, a crescent moon at her feet and a half-hidden scroll in her lap. Behind her hangs a veil sewn with pomegranates, with water just visible beyond it. She asks you to slow down and listen to what you already sense, even if you cannot yet put it into words.

Upright meaning

Intuition, mystery, the inner voice.

Reversed meaning

Secrets, ignoring your intuition.
