Fox Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 14 of 36 · playing-card insert 9♣

cunning, caution, self-interest

Meaning

The Fox is clever and looks after itself first. Traditionally it warns of tricks, false friends or something that is not what it claims, and in modern readings it often stands for work and the skills you use to get by. It asks you to trust your instincts and check the details.

In combination

It casts doubt on its neighbor, suggesting something disguised, strategic or worth a second look.
