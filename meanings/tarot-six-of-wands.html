Six of Wands Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Wands

recognition, progress, confidence

The card

A rider on a white horse wears a laurel wreath, and another wreath is tied to the top of his wand. People walk alongside him carrying their own wands, as if in a small parade. The card speaks of progress that others can see, and invites you to accept praise graciously and share the credit.

Upright meaning

Victory and public recognition; your progress is seen and your confidence is earned.

Reversed meaning

Success goes unnoticed, or the need for applause starts to drive the choices.
