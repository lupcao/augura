Eight of Wands Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Wands

movement, speed, focus

The card

Eight wands fly through clear air in parallel lines, slanting down toward open country and a river below. There are no people on the card, only motion. It speaks of a moment when things move fast, and it invites you to aim your energy in one direction and let it travel.

Upright meaning

Things move fast now: news arrives, plans take flight and momentum carries you forward.

Reversed meaning

Haste causes mistakes, or progress stalls and messages go astray.
