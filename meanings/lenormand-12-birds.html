Birds Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 12 of 36 · playing-card insert 7♦

conversation, nerves, pairs

Meaning

The Birds are chatter: conversations, phone calls, gossip and a flutter of nervous excitement. Traditionally they also stand for couples, siblings or two of something. They ask you to notice what is being said around you and whether worry is getting louder than it needs to be.

In combination

It turns its neighbor into talk, a conversation about it or a little nervous energy around it.
