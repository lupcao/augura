Ace of Swords Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Swords

clarity, breakthrough, truth, a sharp idea

The card

A hand reaches out of a cloud holding a single upright sword, its tip passing through a crown hung with green sprigs, high above bare gray peaks. It is the moment a thought cuts cleanly through the fog. The card asks you to name what you now see plainly and to use that clarity fairly.

Upright meaning

A breakthrough of clarity; the truth cuts through confusion and a sharp new idea emerges.

Reversed meaning

Muddled thinking or a truth used to wound; clarity is clouded or misused.
