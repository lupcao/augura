Ten of Wands Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Wands

burden, responsibility, completion

The card

A man bends forward under a bundle of ten wands, his face hidden behind them as he trudges toward a town. He has nearly arrived, but the load has become all he can see. The card invites you to look at what you are carrying, finish what matters and set down what was never yours to hold.

Upright meaning

You carry too much at once; success has become a burden and some of it can be set down.

Reversed meaning

The load finally slips: delegate, drop what is not yours, or risk a collapse.
