Clouds Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 6 of 36 · playing-card insert K♣

confusion, doubt, passing trouble

Meaning

The Clouds bring confusion, doubt and a stretch of unclear weather. Traditionally the card has a light side and a dark side, and the cards on the darker side are the ones most clouded, but either way the weather passes. It asks you to wait for a clearer view before deciding.

In combination

It clouds its neighbor, making it uncertain, unclear or hard to read for now.
