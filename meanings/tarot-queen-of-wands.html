Queen of Wands Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Wands

warmth, confidence, self-assurance

The card

A queen sits on a throne carved with lions, a wand in one hand and a sunflower in the other, with a black cat at her feet. She looks out with open, sunny self-possession. The card invites you to lead with warmth and trust your own light, steady enough in yourself to make others feel welcome.

Upright meaning

Warm, magnetic and sure of herself; confidence and generosity light up those around you.

Reversed meaning

Self-doubt, jealousy or a fiery temper eats into that natural warmth.
