Eight of Cups Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Cups

walking away, seeking more, moving on

The card

Under a watchful moon, a cloaked figure with a staff walks away from eight neatly stacked cups toward rocky hills. Nothing looks broken, yet something is missing from the arrangement. The card speaks of the courage to leave what no longer fulfills you and to focus on a deeper search, even when the road ahead is unclear.

Upright meaning

You walk away from something that no longer satisfies to search for deeper meaning.

Reversed meaning

Fear keeps you in a situation you have outgrown, or you drift without a direction.
