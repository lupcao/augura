Dog Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 18 of 36 · playing-card insert 10♥

friendship, loyalty, trust

Meaning

The Dog is a loyal friend: someone you can count on, a companion or a helper. Traditionally it stands for faithfulness and support given freely. It asks who is truly on your side and whom you stand by.

In combination

It makes its neighbor friendly and reliable, or points to a friend involved in the matter.
