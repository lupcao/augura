Two of Wands Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Wands

planning, choice, wider horizons

The card

A man stands on the walls of his castle, a small globe in one hand and a wand in the other, a second wand fixed beside him. He looks out over sea and land, weighing what he already has against what lies beyond. The card invites you to choose a direction and balance comfort against the pull of something bigger.

Upright meaning

You hold the world in your hand and weigh a bigger plan; it is time to decide where to go next.

Reversed meaning

Fear of the unknown keeps you planning instead of moving, or comfort keeps you from choosing.
