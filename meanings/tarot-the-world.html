The World Tarot Card Meaning

Major Arcana, number XXI

completion, wholeness, achievement, integration

The card

A dancer wrapped loosely in a purple sash floats inside a large oval wreath, holding a wand in each hand. In the four corners are an angel, an eagle, a lion and a bull, the same figures seen on the Wheel of Fortune. The card marks a cycle brought to a full close and asks you to take in how far you have come before the next journey begins.

Upright meaning

Completion, wholeness, achievement.

Reversed meaning

Unfinished business, delay.
