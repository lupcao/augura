Hexagram 37: The Family

䷤ 家人 (Jiā Rén) · The family. Order starts at home with clear roles.

home, roles, care, trust

Lower trigram: Fire ☲ · Upper trigram: Wind ☴

The judgment

Order and warmth begin at home, in the closest circle of people you share daily life with. When each person knows their part and keeps their word, the whole household holds steady. The quiet, faithful care of whoever tends the inner life of the home is what makes it work.

The image

Wind rising from Fire: the warmth of the hearth sends its influence outward. Say things that mean something and keep your habits steady, so what you say at home matches how you live.

The six lines

Read only the lines that changed in your casting, counting from the bottom.

  • Line 1 (bottom): Set clear, kind limits within the family early, before habits harden. Doing it now spares regret later.
  • Line 2: Rather than chasing your own whims, attend to the everyday nourishment of the household. That steady attention is the heart of things here.
  • Line 3: If the household runs hot and you come down too hard, you may regret it, yet that does less harm than letting everything slide into giggling chaos. Find the balance between firmness and ease.
  • Line 4: Someone who keeps the household well provided for and well tended enriches everyone in it. Quiet stewardship brings real good.
  • Line 5: Lead the family the way a good ruler would, through love rather than fear. When trust runs both ways, there is nothing to dread.
  • Line 6 (top): Your conduct earns respect because it is sincere. Over the long run, a person of real character gives the whole home its footing.
