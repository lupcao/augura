Rider Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 1 of 36 · playing-card insert 9♥

news, speed, arrival

Meaning

The Rider brings news, a visitor or something arriving at speed. Traditionally it is read as movement that starts outside you and reaches you soon: a message, an invitation, a first step taken. It asks what you are ready to receive.

In combination

It makes the neighboring card arrive quickly or as news, so Rider beside Letter reads as a message on its way.
