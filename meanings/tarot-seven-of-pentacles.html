Seven of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Pentacles

patience, investment, taking stock, slow growth

The card

A young farmer rests on his hoe and gazes at a leafy bush growing seven coins, as if weighing what all his work has produced. The harvest is not quite ready. The card invites a pause to take stock of your efforts, to be patient with slow growth and to decide where your energy is best spent next.

Upright meaning

Pause and take stock of your long-term investment; growth is slow but real.

Reversed meaning

Impatience with slow results, or effort poured into something that will not pay off.
