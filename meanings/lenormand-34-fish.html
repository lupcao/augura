Fish Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 34 of 36 · playing-card insert K♦

abundance, flow, independence

Meaning

The Fish stand for abundance and flow: resources, trade, independence and plenty. Traditionally they are read around business and material matters, and also around depth and freedom of movement. They ask what you have in plenty and how freely it flows.

In combination

It adds flow and abundance to its neighbor, something independent, plentiful or tied to resources.
