Stork Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 17 of 36 · playing-card insert Q♥

change, movement, renewal

Meaning

The Stork brings change and improvement, often by moving: a new home, a new phase, a return. Traditionally it is the bird that carries something new into life. It asks what change you are ready to welcome.

In combination

It brings change to its neighbor, an improvement, a move or something returning in a new form.
