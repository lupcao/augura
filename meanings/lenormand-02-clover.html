Clover Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 2 of 36 · playing-card insert 6♦

luck, chance, lightness

Meaning

Clover is a small piece of luck: a lucky break, a second chance, a reason to smile. Its luck is traditionally short-lived, so it is read as an opening to take while it is there rather than a lasting state. It invites you to keep things light and say yes to the easy opportunity.

In combination

It softens and brightens its neighbor, adding a touch of luck or making a difficulty feel briefer.
