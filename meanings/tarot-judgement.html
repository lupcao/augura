Judgement Tarot Card Meaning

Major Arcana, number XX

reckoning, calling, rebirth, forgiveness

The card

An angel blows a trumpet from the clouds, and below, men, women and children rise from their coffins with arms open to greet the call. Snow-capped mountains stand behind them. The card asks you to look back on your past with honesty and kindness, and to answer a call that feels like a fresh start.

Upright meaning

Reckoning, a calling, rebirth.

Reversed meaning

Self-doubt, ignoring the call.
