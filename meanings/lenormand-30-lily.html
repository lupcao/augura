Lily Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 30 of 36 · playing-card insert K♠

peace, maturity, grace

Meaning

The Lily is calm and maturity: peace, harmony, experience and a quiet sense of virtue. Traditionally it can stand for an older, wiser person or a later stage of life, and also for a sensuality that is gentle rather than urgent. It asks where you could bring more patience and grace.

In combination

It calms and matures its neighbor, lending it peace, experience or a slower, more graceful pace.
