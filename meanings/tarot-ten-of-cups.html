Ten of Cups Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Cups

harmony, family, fulfillment

The card

A couple stands with arms around each other, raising their hands toward a rainbow of ten cups, while two children dance beside them. A small house sits among green hills. The card speaks of lasting emotional fulfillment, and invites you to notice the everyday moments where love feels like home.

Upright meaning

Lasting happiness and harmony at home; love shared with family and friends.

Reversed meaning

Tension at home or a gap between the ideal family picture and real life.
