Ring Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 25 of 36 · playing-card insert A♣

commitment, agreement, cycle

Meaning

The Ring is a commitment: a promise, a partnership, an agreement or a bond that holds. Traditionally it is read as union, and also as cycles that come round again. It asks what you are bound to and whether you still choose it.

In combination

It binds its neighbor, making it committed, agreed upon or part of a repeating cycle.
