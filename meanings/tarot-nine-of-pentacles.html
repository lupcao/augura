Nine of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Pentacles

self-reliance, comfort, enjoyment, earned ease

The card

A woman in a robe patterned with flowers stands in a lush vineyard heavy with grapes, a hooded falcon resting on her gloved hand and a fine house behind her. Nine coins gleam among the vines. She has made a good life through her own effort and enjoys it on her own terms. The card invites you to savor what you have built and to trust your independence.

Upright meaning

Self-made comfort and independence; you enjoy the rewards of your own effort.

Reversed meaning

Overspending, dependence on others, or comfort that feels lonely.
