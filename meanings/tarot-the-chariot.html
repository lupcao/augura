The Chariot Tarot Card Meaning

Major Arcana, number VII

determination, drive, willpower, victory

The card

A crowned charioteer stands in a canopied chariot pulled by two sphinxes, one black and one white, with a walled city behind him. He holds no reins and steers by will alone. The card asks you to bring together the parts of you that pull in different directions and move forward with purpose.

Upright meaning

Determination, victory, drive.

Reversed meaning

Lack of direction, aggression.
