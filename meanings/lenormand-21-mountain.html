Mountain Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 21 of 36 · playing-card insert 8♣

obstacle, delay, endurance

Meaning

The Mountain is an obstacle: a delay, a hard climb or something that will not move. Traditionally it is read as patience being tested, but also as the strength that grows from the effort. It asks whether to climb, go around or wait.

In combination

It slows or blocks its neighbor, adding delay, difficulty or distance.
