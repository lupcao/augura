Knight of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Pentacles

reliability, routine, patience, hard work

The card

A knight sits on a heavy, motionless horse at the edge of a plowed field, holding a coin and regarding it calmly. Unlike the other knights, he is not charging anywhere. He stands for steady, dependable effort, and the card asks you to keep going methodically while making sure routine does not turn into a rut.

Upright meaning

Slow, reliable and thorough; steady routine and hard work get the job done.

Reversed meaning

Stuck in a rut, overly cautious or working without any joy.
