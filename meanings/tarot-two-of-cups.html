Two of Cups Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Cups

partnership, connection, mutual respect

The card

A young man and woman face each other and exchange cups, as if making a promise. Above them floats a staff entwined with two snakes and crowned with a winged lion's head. The card speaks of a meeting of equals, and asks you to notice what a relationship needs in order to stay balanced and kind.

Upright meaning

A bond of mutual attraction and respect; two people meet as equals.

Reversed meaning

Imbalance or a falling-out between partners, or communication that has broken down.
