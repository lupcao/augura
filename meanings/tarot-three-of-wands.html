Three of Wands Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Wands

expansion, foresight, first results

The card

Seen from behind, a figure stands on a high ridge among three planted wands, watching ships move across the water below. Plans have been set in motion and the first of them are already under way. The card asks you to look further ahead and welcome help from others as your efforts begin to bear fruit.

Upright meaning

Your efforts are underway and the first ships are coming in; look ahead and keep expanding.

Reversed meaning

Progress is slower than hoped, or plans were too small or too vague to carry far.
