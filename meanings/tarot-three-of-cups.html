Three of Cups Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Cups

friendship, community, joy

The card

Three women dance in a circle and raise their cups in a toast, with fruit and flowers from the harvest at their feet. Their joy comes from being together. The card invites you to share good moments with the people who stand by you, and to notice what grows when you work and celebrate side by side.

Upright meaning

Friendship, celebration and shared joy; a time to gather and toast what is good.

Reversed meaning

Too much indulgence, gossip, or feeling left out of the circle.
