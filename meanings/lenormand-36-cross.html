Cross Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 36 of 36 · playing-card insert 6♣

burden, duty, meaning

Meaning

The Cross is a burden carried: duty, hardship and the weight of what matters. Traditionally it is one of the heavier cards, read as a trial that belongs to one's path, and also as faith and deeper meaning. It asks what you are carrying and what gives that weight its purpose.

In combination

It adds weight to its neighbor, a burden, a duty or something that matters deeply.
