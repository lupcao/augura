The Fool Tarot Card Meaning

Major Arcana, number 0

beginnings, trust, spontaneity

The card

A young traveler steps toward the edge of a cliff with eyes on the sky, a small bundle over one shoulder, a white rose in hand and a little dog at their heels. The sun is bright and the whole road lies ahead. The card asks what you would try if you trusted yourself a little more, and whether your next step deserves a quick glance at the ground first.

Upright meaning

New beginnings, a leap of faith.

Reversed meaning

Recklessness, hesitation.
