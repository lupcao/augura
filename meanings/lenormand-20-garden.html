Garden Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 20 of 36 · playing-card insert 8♠

public, gathering, community

Meaning

The Garden is the public sphere: gatherings, parties, social circles and anything that happens in the open. Traditionally it is where you meet people beyond your home and are seen by others. It asks how you show up among people and where you feel you belong.

In combination

It makes its neighbor public or social, something shared, seen or happening among a group.
