Key Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 33 of 36 · playing-card insert 8♦

solution, certainty, opening

Meaning

The Key is the solution: something unlocks, a door opens, a matter becomes certain. Traditionally it is read as a firm yes, and it marks what matters most in a reading. It asks what you already hold that could open the way.

In combination

It makes its neighbor important and certain, the key point or the thing that unlocks the matter.
