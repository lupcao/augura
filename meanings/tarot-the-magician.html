The Magician Tarot Card Meaning

Major Arcana, number I

willpower, skill, focus, resourcefulness

The card

A figure stands at a table, one hand raising a wand toward the sky and the other pointing down to the earth. On the table lie a cup, a sword, a pentacle and a wand, and an infinity sign floats above his head. The card asks you to notice the tools already within reach and to put your attention where your intention is.

Upright meaning

Willpower, skill, manifestation.

Reversed meaning

Manipulation, untapped talent.
