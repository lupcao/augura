Three of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Pentacles

teamwork, craft, collaboration, skill

The card

Inside a cathedral, a stonemason stands on a bench carving an arch while a monk and a figure holding the plans look on and talk it over, three coins worked into the stone above. Each brings a different skill to the same task. The card celebrates good craft and collaboration, and asks you to value what others add as the work takes shape.

Upright meaning

Teamwork and craftsmanship; skills combine and the work is recognized.

Reversed meaning

Poor coordination, mediocre work or a team that does not listen to each other.
