The Lovers Tarot Card Meaning

Major Arcana, number VI

union, values, choice, connection

The card

A man and a woman stand unclothed beneath a winged angel and a blazing sun. Behind the woman grows a tree with a serpent wound around it, and behind the man a tree of flames. The card speaks of closeness and of choosing in line with what you truly value, in love and well beyond it.

Upright meaning

Union, shared values, a choice of the heart.

Reversed meaning

Disharmony, misaligned values.
