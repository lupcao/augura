Crossroads Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 22 of 36 · playing-card insert Q♦

choice, options, paths

Meaning

The Crossroads is a choice between paths. Traditionally it shows options, alternatives and the freedom, sometimes uncomfortable, of having to decide. It asks you to weigh the roads in front of you and pick one.

In combination

It adds choice to its neighbor, showing more than one option or a decision that has to be made about it.
