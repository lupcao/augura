Page of Cups Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Cups

imagination, sensitivity, pleasant surprise

The card

A young figure in a flower-patterned tunic stands by the sea, looking at a cup from which a small fish peeks out. The page seems delighted rather than alarmed. The card brings a gentle surprise, a creative idea or a tender message, and invites you to stay open to feelings that arrive in unexpected ways.

Upright meaning

A dreamy, sensitive messenger brings a sweet surprise, a creative idea or an emotional opening.

Reversed meaning

Emotional immaturity, moodiness, or a creative idea that stays a daydream.
