Ten of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Pentacles

legacy, family, lasting wealth, roots

The card

In the foreground an old man in a richly patterned cloak sits with two dogs, while a couple talk beneath a stone archway and a child reaches out to one of the dogs, ten coins laid over the scene in a careful pattern. Three generations share one home. The card speaks of roots, family and what lasts beyond a single lifetime, and asks what you would like to pass on.

Upright meaning

Lasting wealth, family roots and a legacy that supports the generations.

Reversed meaning

Family disputes about money, or security that turns out to be fragile.
