Five of Pentacles Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Pentacles

hardship, feeling left out, struggle, help nearby

The card

Two figures in rags trudge through falling snow, one on crutches, past a church window glowing with five coins in its stained glass. They are cold and fixed on the struggle, and do not seem to notice the light just above them. The card acknowledges hard times and feeling shut out, and gently points to support that may be closer than it looks.

Upright meaning

Hard times and feeling left out in the cold, yet help is closer than it seems.

Reversed meaning

Recovery from hardship begins, or you finally accept the help on offer.
