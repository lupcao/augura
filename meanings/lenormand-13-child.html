Child Lenormand Card Meaning

Card 13 of 36 · playing-card insert J♠

new start, innocence, curiosity

Meaning

The Child is something new and small: a fresh start, a young person or an open, curious attitude. Traditionally it is read as innocence and trust, and sometimes as inexperience. It asks you to approach the matter with beginner's eyes.

In combination

It makes its neighbor new, young or small, a first step rather than the finished thing.
