Page of Swords Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Swords

curiosity, vigilance, new ideas, sharp questions

The card

A young person stands on a windswept rise, sword raised in both hands, glancing sideways as clouds race and birds scatter overhead. They are alert, curious and eager to test every idea. The card encourages fresh questions and quick learning, with a reminder that a sharp tongue still needs a kind aim.

Upright meaning

Curious and alert, eager to learn and quick to ask sharp questions.

Reversed meaning

Gossip, all talk and no action, or ideas that scatter before they land.
