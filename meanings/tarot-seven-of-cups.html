Seven of Cups Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Cups

daydreams, options, illusion

The card

A dark figure faces seven cups floating on a cloud, each holding a different vision: a face, a veiled glowing figure, a snake, a castle, jewels, a laurel wreath and a dragon. Some look like treasures and some like warnings. The card asks you to step back and assess which dreams are real possibilities and which are only daydreams.

Upright meaning

Many tempting options float before you, but some are illusions; choose with clear eyes.

Reversed meaning

The fog lifts: you see through the fantasy and commit to one real choice.
