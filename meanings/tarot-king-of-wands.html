King of Wands Tarot Card Meaning

Minor Arcana, suit of Wands

vision, leadership, initiative

The card

A king sits on a throne decorated with lions and salamanders, holding a flowering wand, while a small salamander rests at his feet. He leans slightly forward, as if already seeing the next move. The card speaks of seasoned, inspiring leadership and asks you to hold a clear vision and carry it through with integrity.

Upright meaning

A visionary leader who inspires others and turns big ideas into reality.

Reversed meaning

Domineering or impulsive leadership; vision without patience for other people.
